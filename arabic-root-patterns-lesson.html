١ · الأساس

الجذر (The Root)

Most Arabic words are built from a three-letter root that carries the core meaning. Every word from the same root is a relative — they share meaning and often shape.

خذ الجذر ك‑ت‑ب ومعناه العام "الكتابة". من هذا الجذر نحصل على:

WordMeaningType
كَتَبَhe wroteverb
كِتابbooknoun
كاتِبwriteractive participle
مَكْتَبoffice / desknoun of place
مَكْتَبةlibrarynoun of place

Today's lesson focuses on the last group: words built to mean a place, a time, or a tool — all derived from a root using a fixed template (وزن).

٢ · اسم المكان والزمان

اسم المكان والزمان (Noun of Place & Time)

Same pattern is used for "the place where X happens" and "the time when X happens." From a regular triliteral verb, two shapes are common:

مَفْعَل most verbs — middle letter takes fatha
مَفْعِل verbs whose present-tense middle letter takes kasra
مَفْعَلة feminine ending — often for enclosed / building-like places

٣ · اسم الآلة

اسم الآلة (Noun of Instrument)

The word for "the tool used to do X." Four common shapes — the last one is the modern pattern used for machines.

مِفْعَلe.g. مِفْتاح — key
مِفْعَلةe.g. مِكْنَسة — broom
مِفْعالe.g. مِنْشار — saw
فَعّالةmodern machines, e.g. غَسّالة — washing machine
